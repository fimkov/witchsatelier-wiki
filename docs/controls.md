# Controls

Witch's Atelier adds one key of its own: **Close the ring**, ++v++ by default. You can rebind it under
Options, Controls, Key Binds, *Witch's Atelier*. Everything else uses the game's own Use, Sneak and Jump.

## Drawing

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Draw on a surface | Hold Use with a loaded pen and move your view: the pen follows where you look |
| Rub out | Sneak while drawing with the pen |
| Wipe ink away | Hold Use with a rag on a surface and move your view over the lines |
| Draw a perfect ring | Go round twice in the air without lifting the pen; the third time round lays it down (after reading *The Imagined Ring*) |
| Draw on a page | Paper or a palm quire in the other hand, Use in the air |

## Ink

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Dip the pen | Use the pen on a placed inkwell, or hold an inkwell in your other hand while drawing |
| Fill an inkwell | Click ink bottles onto it in your inventory, or use them on a placed inkwell |
| Make blood ink | Sneak and use a bottle of conjuring ink (forbidden, costs health) |

## Pages and books

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Cast a prepared page | Hold the page and press ++v++: its ring closes and the spell fires where you look |
| Open a palm quire | Use it |
| Tear a page out of a palm quire | ++t++ while it is open |
| Put pages into a palm quire | Click paper or crumpled pages onto it in your inventory |
| Show a grimoire glyph for tracing | Hold the grimoire with a pen in the other hand: the glyph shows where you look |
| Size it / pin it | Scroll to size it, sneak to pin it, then trace over it with the pen |

## Artifacts

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Wake a dormant artifact | Artifact in one hand, loaded pen in the other, use in the air and trace the glyph shown on it |
| Restore a faded seal | Use an inked pen on the contraption |
| Fly on sylph shoes | Jump twice to take off; jump to rise, sneak to sink; jump twice again or touch the ground to land |
| Glide with a wingcloak | Leap, then spread it to glide |
| Hide in a mirror mantle | Crouch and keep still |
| Part water or lava | Use the Raincleaver towards it; sneak and use on the ground to plant it |
| Follow a light tracer bangle | Hold Use |
| Ride a sealchair | Sit in it and look where you want it to walk |
