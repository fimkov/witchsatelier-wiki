# Fire

Spells of the Fire sigil: flame that rises, flies, fans out, lies in wait, or only warms.

## Snare of Flame

Closed, the glyph wakes but holds its fire: the pillar bursts up only when a creature steps onto it. Its maker can walk over it safely.</p><p>Tread holds back any glyph this way. Drawn in invisible ink, nobody sees the snare before it springs.</p><p>Close a Tread glyph while someone stands in it and it remembers them: from then on only they can press it. Turn the Tread inward and it works the other way round, awake while nobody stands on it and quiet while it is pressed.</p><p>It burns for as long as something stands on it, or while the block it is drawn on is powered by redstone. About two seconds after it is let go it waits again, ready for the next step. See <a href="../../glyphs/#chains-and-triggers">Chains and triggers</a>.</p></div><div class="found">Found in <a href="../../books/#of-fire-and-hearth">Of Fire and Hearth</a></div></div>
