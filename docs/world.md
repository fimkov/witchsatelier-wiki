# The world

## The Passage Stars

About once every 24 days, on the first clear night it is due, the **Passage Stars** cross the overworld sky: a meteor shower of a few minutes, running down to the horizon. Anyone under the open sky while they fall has seen them. The first shower comes somewhere between the sixth and the twelfth day of a world.

For three days after, crops and berries around players ripen faster: the harvest after the stars.

The game rule `witchsatelierPassageStars` turns new showers off.
