# Creatures

## Brimhat

<div class="card mini-row"><img class="icon big" src="../assets/icons/brimhat_mask.png" alt=""><div><p>Witches of the forbidden path, faces hidden behind porcelain masks, brims pulled low. They keep their distance and never fight fair. Found in forests, and more often in swamps, mangrove swamps and dark forests.</p></div></div>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Health | 30 |
| Armor | 2 |
| Behaviour | Hostile. Keeps a wary distance, closes in when you are far, backs off when you are close |

A Brimhat draws glyphs in the air with a quick hand, every few seconds while it can see you:

- **Fire bolts**: a volley of two to four fireballs.
- **Binding**: a ring of red threads opens under your feet and holds you in place.
- **Shadow step**: it vanishes in smoke and reappears beside you, most often when you get too close.
- **Curse**: forbidden magic drawn onto your shadow: darkness, weakness and slowness for a few seconds.

**Drops**

<div class="flow"><span class="step"><img class="icon" src="../assets/icons/conjuring_ink.png" alt=""><span>Conjuring Ink</span></span><span class="step"><img class="icon" src="../assets/icons/blood_ink.png" alt=""><span>Blood Ink</span></span><span class="step"><img class="icon" src="../assets/icons/torn_page.png" alt=""><span>Forbidden page</span></span><span class="step"><img class="icon" src="../assets/icons/brimhat_mask.png" alt=""><span>Mask (rare)</span></span><span class="step"><img class="icon" src="../assets/icons/brimhat_mantle.png" alt=""><span>Mantle (rarer)</span></span><span class="step"><img class="icon" src="../assets/icons/grimoire.png" alt=""><span>Black-Bound Book (rare)</span></span></div>

The forbidden page carries the [Petrification](spells/forbidden.md) glyph, its ring left open. The
[Black-Bound Book](books.md#the-black-bound-book) is the forbidden book itself. Brimhats leave
anyone wearing a Brimhat mask alone. The Knights Moralis hunt Brimhats on sight.

## Knight Moralis

<div class="card mini-row"><img class="icon big" src="../assets/icons/capture_pennant.png" alt=""><div><p>The order that enforces the Pact. Silver-white, unhurried, polite to the innocent. Each knight carries an iron sword and a silver-white capture pennant.</p></div></div>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Health | 44 |
| Armor | 10 |
| Attack | 7 |
| Behaviour | Neutral unless you have broken the Pact, wear a Brimhat mask or strike it first. Hostile to Brimhats |

A knight first pins its target with a ring of binding light, then closes in with the blade. A witch it hunts, bound
and worn down, it would rather take alive: its pennant turns black and erases instead of killing
([Sinsinger's Talons](pact.md#the-knights-hunt)). Knights step out of thin
air near the one they are sent for, and when walking gets them no closer (water, walls, a tower of blocks) they step
across. When their work is done they turn away, walk off a little and vanish in a fold of light.

**Drops:** rarely the iron sword, rarely the [Capture Pennant](artifacts.md#silver-white-capture-pennant).

How and when the knights come is on [The Pact](pact.md#the-knights-hunt).

## Stranger

<div class="card mini-row"><img class="icon big" src="../assets/icons/grimoire.png" alt=""><div><p>A silent figure in a broad-brimmed hat and a long dark mantle. Very rarely, while you are in a village, the Stranger passes by and stays about a day.</p></div></div>

The first time you speak to them, they press a gift into your hands without a word:

<div class="flow"><span class="step"><img class="icon" src="../assets/icons/grimoire.png" alt=""><span>A Witch's Primer</span></span><span class="step"><img class="icon" src="../assets/icons/ink_pen.png" alt=""><span>Ink Pen</span></span><span class="step"><img class="icon" src="../assets/icons/conjuring_ink.png" alt=""><span>Conjuring Ink</span></span></div>

The [Primer](books.md#a-witchs-primer) explains how glyphs work and holds Lantern, Birds of Light and Pillar of
Flame, and on its last page, in blood ink, a glyph that should never have been in it. After that, the Stranger
trades for emeralds:

| Item | Emeralds |
| --- | --- |
| A themed volume, one at random (see [Books](books.md#themed-volumes)) | 12 |
| Book of Contraptions | 8 |
| Notes on the Imagined Ring | 16 |
| Crumpled page with a prepared glyph (two offers) | 2 each |
| Ink Pen | 4 |
| Conjuring Ink | 3 |
| Waterproof Ink | 5 |
| Palm Quire | 6 |
| Silverwood Sapling | 8 |
| Blackened Silverwood Twig (x4) | 2 |

The **Book of Contraptions** explains the [artifacts](artifacts.md), drawing tools, linked glyphs and forbidden magic.
