# Creatures

## Brimhat

Witches of the forbidden path, faces hidden behind porcelain masks, brims pulled low. Brimhats appear in
forests, and more often in swamps, mangrove swamps and dark forests.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Health | 30 |
| Armor | 2 |
| Behaviour | Hostile. Keeps its distance, closes in when you are far, backs off when you are close |

A Brimhat draws glyphs in the air with a quick hand, every few seconds while it can see you:

- **Fire bolts**: a volley of two to four fireballs.
- **Binding**: a ring of red threads opens under your feet and holds you in place.
- **Shadow step**: it vanishes in smoke and reappears beside you. It does this most when you get too close.
- **Curse**: forbidden magic drawn onto your shadow: darkness, weakness and slowness for a few seconds.

**Drops:** conjuring ink, sometimes blood ink, sometimes a page torn from a forbidden book (with the
Petrification glyph, its ring left open), rarely the **Brimhat Mask** and, more rarely, the **Brimhat Mantle**.

Brimhats leave anyone wearing a Brimhat mask alone. The Knights Moralis hunt Brimhats on sight.

## Knight Moralis

The order that enforces the Pact. Silver-white, unhurried, polite to the innocent. Knights carry an iron sword
and a capture pennant.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Health | 44 |
| Armor | 10 |
| Attack | 7 |
| Behaviour | Neutral unless you have broken the Pact, wear a Brimhat mask or strike it first. Hostile to Brimhats |

A knight first pins its target with a ring of binding light, then closes in with the blade. Knights step
out of thin air near the one they are sent for, and when walking gets them no closer (water, walls, a tower of
blocks) they step across. When their work is done they turn away, walk off a little and vanish in a fold of light.

**Drops:** rarely the iron sword, rarely the **Silver-White Capture Pennant**.

How and when the knights come is explained in [The Pact](pact.md#the-knights-hunt).

## Stranger

A silent figure in a broad-brimmed hat and a long dark mantle. Very rarely, while you are in a village, the
Stranger passes by and stays about a day, never while another Stranger is nearby.

The first time you speak to them, they press a gift into your hands without a word:

- an **Old Grimoire** that opens with Birds of Light, Pillar of Flame, Lantern, Wind Underfoot and
  Spilling Water, and ends with a page that should never have been in it;
- an **Ink Pen**;
- a bottle of **Conjuring Ink**.

After that they trade for emeralds:

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Old Grimoire (four to six glyphs) | 12 |
| Book of Contraptions | 8 |
| Crumpled page with a prepared glyph (two offers) | 2 each |
| Ink Pen | 4 |
| Conjuring Ink | 3 |
| Waterproof Ink | 5 |
| Palm Quire | 6 |
| Silverwood Sapling | 8 |
| Blackened Silverwood Twig (x4) | 2 |

The **Book of Contraptions** explains contraptions, the artifacts on the [Artifacts](artifacts.md) page,
drawing tools, linked glyphs and forbidden magic.
