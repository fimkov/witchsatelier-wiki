# How glyphs work

> A glyph is a ring with a sigil in its middle and signs around it. The sigil is the element; the signs say what it
> does. Nothing happens until the ring is closed, so witches leave a small gap and close it when they need the spell.
>
> *On Glyphs, the first page of every grimoire*

The symbols themselves are on [Sigils and signs](reference.md); every known glyph is under [Spells](spells/index.md).

## The rules

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Size** | A bigger ring is a stronger spell. |
| **Neatness** | A neat hand makes a stronger glyph that wears more slowly. |
| **Ink** | Some inks are stronger, last longer or resist water. See [Inks](inks.md). |
| **Wear** | A glyph works for as long as it is whole. Rain washes ordinary ink away, feet scuff it, a rag rubs it out. |
| **Orientation** | A sign drawn the other way round does the opposite: Levitation presses down, Pull pushes, Crush mends. |
| **Counting** | Some signs count: each Bolt is one fireball, each Bird is one bird. |
| **Mixing** | Two kinds of shaping in one ring work side by side, if each is drawn at least twice. A single stray sign does nothing. |
| **Surface** | A glyph on a wall throws its spell sideways. Earth magic needs a surface: from a page, look at the block. |

## Nested glyphs

A ring inside a ring shapes the spell within. Draw a whole glyph, ring it again, and put signs in the band between
the two rings: **Repetition** keeps the glyph from wearing away, **Enlarge** makes it stronger, **Billowing** makes
everything around fall soft. A sigil drawn in the band adds its element to the spell inside. Nothing works until
the **outer** ring is closed. See [Nested glyphs](spells/nested.md).

## Linked glyphs

Glyphs joined by a line of ink are one work. Draw a line from the rim of one ring to the rim of another: when one
wakes, every glyph joined to it wakes too, even one whose own ring was left open. Each glyph in the chain makes all
the others stronger, so many small linked glyphs beat one big one. The Raincleaver's blade is covered in them.

Draw the Window sign alone in a ring on a wall, floor or door, then another somewhere else: they join, and you can
step into one and out of the other. A new window joins the last of yours still waiting. Joined windows share a rim
colour; a waiting one stays dark grey. Make the ring at least two and a half blocks across, or you will not fit.

## Sylph shoes

<div class="card-media wide"><video autoplay muted loop playsinline preload="metadata" poster="../assets/clips/sylph_boots.jpg"><source src="../assets/clips/sylph_boots.webm" type="video/webm"></video></div>

Draw [Wind Underfoot](spells/wind.md#wind-underfoot) on the soles of any boots: hold the boots in one hand and the
pen in the other, and the glyph shows faintly to be traced, split across both soles. While you wear them, click your
heels (jump twice) to fly as in creative: jump to rise, sneak to sink. Click them again, or touch the ground, to land.
Nothing flies under water, and water washes the glyph off unless its ink is waterproof.

## Ships

With **Create Aeronautics** (through Sable) installed, ships feel magic like everything else. A levitation glyph
under a ship lifts it off the ground and holds it up; reversed, it lets the ship sink gently. Wind spells push it.

Draw the flight glyph on a ship's underside and stand on its deck, and the ship goes where you look. Crouch and it
hangs still in the air.

## Contraptions

A glyph drawn onto a thing makes the thing do magic by itself: the only way people who are not witches can use magic
at all. Some hold their seal for good; most wear out and need a witch to restore them with pen and ink.
See [Artifacts](artifacts.md).
