# The Pact

The Pact is the law of witches. It forbids magic worked on living bodies and glyphs that change them,
and it says that no one but a witch may see how magic is made. Break it, and sooner or later the
Knights Moralis come.

## What breaks the Pact

| Act | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Turning a living thing to stone (Petrification) | Very heavy |
| Being seen drawing or casting by villagers | Moderate, once per glyph |
| Drawing with blood ink | Light, but every stroke counts |

Your debt to the Pact fades slowly with time.

## Forbidden magic

### Petrification

Earth under the gaze of four **Eye** signs. Whatever living thing stands before the glyph stiffens and turns
to stone, leaving a statue. Honest grimoires do not carry it; Brimhats do, and pages torn from forbidden books.

Only **Integration** (Earth with four reversed Crush signs) drawn around a statue can give it back.

### Blood ink

The Brimhats' ink. Sneak and use a bottle of conjuring ink to bleed into it. It makes glyphs three times as
strong, with far less control. Every line drawn with it counts against you.

## Villagers who see magic

A villager who is awake, grown, within 16 blocks, facing you and able to see you notices when you draw or cast.
It takes fright and runs from you, thinks worse of you (its trades get dearer, and in the end the village golem
turns on you), and the Pact counts it. A whole glyph drawn in front of villagers counts once, not stroke by stroke.

Draw where no one is watching, behind walls, or with your back to the village.

## The knights' hunt

Once your debt is great enough, you get no warning but a low bell and a feeling of being watched. From then on,
now and then, the order notices, and **Knights Moralis** step out of the air around you.

- The heavier the debt, the more knights come: one, two, or three.
- Running does not help. If you leave the knights behind for about half a minute (outran, hidden, or in
  country they cannot reach), others are sent from where you are now.
- The hunt ends when the debt is paid: when the knights strike you down, or when time has forgiven you enough.
  Dying while you are hunted clears your debt entirely, and the knights go their way.
- Wearing a **Brimhat Mask** fools the Brimhats, but the knights are fooled too, into thinking you are one.
  They attack masked players on sight.

Creative and spectator players are never hunted and are never seen by villagers.
