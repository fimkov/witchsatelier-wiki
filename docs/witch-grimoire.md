A blank book of 16 pages. Use it to open it. Each spread is one page of yours: notes on the left, the glyph on the right.

## Writing in it

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| Left page | Click the top line to name the page, and write your notes below it, straight onto the parchment (up to 320 characters) |
| Right page | Draw with a pen in your other hand. Drag with the right mouse button to rub out |
| Contents tab | Lists your pages by the names you gave them; click one to turn to it |

## Copying glyphs in

- **From the world.** Sneak and use the open book on a glyph drawn on a wall or floor: it is copied onto the current page, scaled to fit and turned the way you see it.
- **From an old grimoire.** Hold an old grimoire in your other hand, open at the page you want, then sneak and use: its glyph is copied in together with its name.

## Using it

Glyphs do not cast from your grimoire, any more than from an old one. It is for keeping and tracing: hold it with a pen in the other hand and the page's glyph shows faintly where you look; scroll to size it, sneak to pin it and trace over it. A faithful tracing casts as the page.

## Copying the book

Put a written grimoire in the crafting grid with one or more blank ones: each blank one becomes a copy, and the original stays. The tooltip of a written grimoire says whom it is **kept by** and how many of its pages are drawn.
